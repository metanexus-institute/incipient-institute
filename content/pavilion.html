Research practice

The site is part
of the study.

Fellows used to keep separate records of footpaths, room use, and visits. In 2018, the board asked operations and research to keep them together. A moved chair, a new path, or a change in the route to the library might look quite different in each set of records.

Staff enter changes in the facilities log. We keep a record of alterations that have no corresponding work order. Dated plans and stills from fixed cameras give fellows a way to compare the same places over time.

We keep earlier records alongside revisions. A discrepancy can be the reason to keep both. A visitor's desk copy and the corresponding carbon sheet may therefore differ.

20 November · 21:10 · Call from the resident

She was at her kitchen table with her son. He had brought his blanket downstairs. While she was on the telephone, they heard him call from his bedroom. He wanted to know why she had moved the chair and opened the door again.

She asked Operations to close the Pavilion door and leave it closed.

The voice in the bedroom had stopped calling for her. The boy at the kitchen table was still holding her hand when he asked when his mother would be home.

She asked us to leave her name out of the handover.
