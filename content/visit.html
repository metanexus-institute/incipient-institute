Visit the Pavilion

Hours and arrival.

The common room and meadow path are open to the public on Saturdays. Members can also use the research library during the week.

If you’re coming with someone who has been here before, the desk may already have both of you on the same line in the register.

When to come.

Admission during public hours is free. The Pavilion is closed on public holidays.

Open to the public

Saturdays, 10 a.m.–4 p.m.

Last entry is at 3:30 p.m. The Open Table begins at noon in the common room.

About the Open Table

Open to members

Thursdays and Fridays, 8 a.m.–8 p.m.

First-time evening visitors will be shown the route to the library. Returning members should check the route posted at the desk, even if they know their way.

Getting around

At the east desk.

The step-free east entrance is reached from the meadow path. On your way out, sign the same line you signed on arrival. The public route includes the common room and accessible washroom. The west hall serves offices and research rooms.

Use the map handed to you when you arrive. The desk keeps earlier copies for visitors who need to describe where they’ve been. If anyone is home, please wait for the guide.

At check-in, visitors sign the visitor agreement, which includes a short nondisclosure agreement and release.

For a copy of your entries or a reply to a correction, use the visitor record desk. Please sign out in person, even if a departure time has already been entered.

Visiting fellows staying overnight can collect their room keys at the same desk. See lodging details.

Getting around

The meadow walk.

Current maps are available at the east desk.

At the east gate

The meadow walk begins at the east gate. If the path starts before you get here, please tell the guide where you first stepped onto it.

Walking with someone

If you walked together and remember coming through different gates, please tell the guide before you compare notes. We can often keep both routes open.

Coming back from the meadow

The loop takes about twenty minutes. If it brings you out in your own garden, please leave the gate open. There may be people behind you.

See the grounds photograph and the board resolution on public routes.

Before your visit

Visitor agreement.

The east desk provides the agreement on arrival. You may ask for a copy to take home.

Confidentiality

Visitors agree to keep other visitors’ names, unpublished research, and material discussed in closed sessions confidential. Please refer requests for comment about an observation under review to the Institute.

Release and withdrawal

The release covers use of notes, route records, and photographs made during your visit. You may withdraw from further contact at any time. Where staff can repeat an observation without contacting you, it remains part of the study. We enter the withdrawal on the date we receive it. An account supplied by another visitor may have an earlier date.

Revised 20 November. The previous edition closed an observation when the visitor’s file was closed. See the archive revisions.

If something has stayed with you since your visit, I’d be grateful to know the first day you noticed it at home.

Thank you for being open.

Jim Lyndon, Chair of the Board

She asked me to close both accounts. I’ve closed hers. His is filed separately, and the desk can still take down anything else he remembers.
