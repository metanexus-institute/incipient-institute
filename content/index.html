The Institute

How places
change us.

We give researchers time, a place to work, and the opportunity to test ideas with people outside their own disciplines. Our fellows study everything from how a path forms across a meadow to how a neighborhood remembers a place that has disappeared.

The Pavilion is part of the research. We keep records of how the rooms, grounds, and people here change over time. The visitor record can include changes first noticed at home. In our annual report, those changes count toward the visit that began them.

If someone who stayed home remembers making the visit with you, please write down what each of you remembers before you compare notes. The east desk can open a file on the account of either person. See the notice for returning visitors.
